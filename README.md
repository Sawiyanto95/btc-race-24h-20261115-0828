Deep-dip ladder cbBTC trading bot for make.fun's 24-hour race: it buys a real dip and rides the bounce. It buys 60% of its cash when the close is 3.0% below the highest close of the last 4 hours, scales in at -4.5% and -6.0%, and exits on a -2% stop, a +4.5% take-profit, or a 1% trailing stop once 0.5% in profit. On the dev window it made +$10.35 net PnL from $1,000 USDC over 10 trades, with 1.9% max drawdown.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
